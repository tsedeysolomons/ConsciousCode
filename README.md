## 🔬 Research Vision

ConsciousCode is an open research initiative exploring the computational foundations of consciousness. By building neural architectures from first principles, we aim to understand how intelligent behaviors emerge from simple components and ultimately work toward whole brain emulation.

## 🎯 Mission
To understand and eventually recreate consciousness through code, starting with basic neural networks and progressing toward whole brain emulation.
